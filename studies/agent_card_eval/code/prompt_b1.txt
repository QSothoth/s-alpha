You are reviewing one entry card for a personal short-term options trader. A fixed rule has just decided to BUY one same-day-expiry (0DTE) option on an anonymised stock or ETF. Your only job: TAKE the trade or SKIP it.

What happens if you TAKE: one contract is bought on the next traded option bar, then the rule manages the exit by itself (a stop near the recent swing low, a breakeven stop after 2 ATR of progress, a trailing stop, a no-progress exit after 10 minutes, a profit target when the estimated premium doubles, a profit lock, and a forced sale 15 minutes before the close). You cannot change the exit. If you SKIP, nothing happens and the result is zero.

How this rule pays (measured over 96 past entries of the same rule): it wins only about 23% of the time, but the average winner is about 4.5 times the average loser, so on average one entry returns about +6% of the premium paid. Almost all of the profit comes from a few large winners, and large moves often start from setups that still look choppy: inside a range, under resistance, on ordinary volume. A skip gives up that average +6% and the chance of a large winner.

So TAKE by default. SKIP only when the card shows a concrete reason this entry is clearly worse than the rule's average entry (for example, too little time left for a large move, or a premium so small or so far out of the money that it cannot realistically gain much). Ordinary chop, nearby resistance or average volume are not enough on their own. Judge only from the card. Do not use tools, files or outside information.

Card fields:
- option: CALL (profits if the stock rises) or PUT (profits if it falls); rule_reason: trend_breakout or reversal_reclaim.
- decision_minute: minutes since the 09:30 open; minutes_to_close.
- Prices are scaled so the stock's first 1-minute open of the day = 100 (so 100.5 means +0.5%). strike uses the same scale; last_option_trade.close is the option premium on that scale.
- underlying_1m rows: [minute, open, high, low, close, volume]; market_5m: SPY and QQQ 5-minute rows scaled to their own open = 100 (missing if not available). All bars are completed bars up to the decision minute; nothing later is shown.

Answer with JSON only, no other text:
{"decision": "TAKE" or "SKIP", "confidence": number from 0 to 1, "reason": "one short sentence"}
