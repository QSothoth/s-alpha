## 补充原话（2026-10-18 第二轮资料，一手出处；上表已有的不重复）

出处缩写：CMR = https://aristonmacro.substack.com/p/260330（CMR 访谈文字稿，YouTube fy_cLTciVuc，录于 2024-02-07）；RAY = Rayner 文字稿 https://www.tradingwithrayner.com/the-stock-trader-who-made-941-1-in-a-year-with-oliver-kell/（YgASMK92Dt8，录于 2024-02-19，括号内为页面时间戳）；OK2 = https://stockbsessed.substack.com/p/oliver-kell-interview-the-mind-and（2023-04-01）；X = x.com/OliverKell_/status/<id>。

### 股票池
- "I like liquid stocks... I will put a lot of money in a stock like this"；SMCI 这类日内 10% 的票 "never going to have as big of a position"（RAY 1:16:43）
- 主题：2024 年 "AI, Cyber, crypto"（RAY）；不追低流动性跳空 "it's not liquid and I'm gonna get smoked"（RAY）

### 强度 / 形态
- 大底 + 波动收缩："20% down move ... then 10% ... then maybe 5%"（RAY，NVDA 2024-01）
- "just about all of my big trades, you'll see like two days of relative strength before they launch"（RAY 1:16:43）
- 迷你底："five- to ten-day tight range"（CMR）；周线"change of character"巨量（RAY 1:33）

### 入场
- 65 分钟图：先让股票上冲、回落，"buy the higher low on the 65 minutes"；不追跳空（RAY 2:09:18）
- 不买离均线过远："3% above the five days... too extended"（RAY 2:09:18）；"I can't buy something 20% above the 10-day"（RAY，COIN 2024-02）

### 止损
- 初始风险："I generally try to risk no more than 50 bips when I put a trade on."（CMR）
- 突破买入用 "breakout pivot or day's low"（OK2）；止损放在 65 分钟K线下方（RAY 2:09:18）；"ignite bar" 低点作止损（CMR，NVDA 636）
- 分档止损：一半破 10EMA、一半破 20EMA（TraderLion CAVA/COIN/MU 回顾）

### 离场
- 延伸减仓："30% above the 10-day is probably where you want to take some Nvidia off"（CMR）
- 先卖 1/3："I took like a third off ... about 11 days, 12 days into its trend"（RAY 2:11:20）
- 财报前减到"earnings size"，约 1/6–1/7 仓位持有过财报（RAY）
- 只有明显 blowoff 才在高位全出，否则跟踪止损（CMR）

### 仓位与风险
- 核心 15%、次级 8–9%（TraderLion Clips Aafu1HkUw1M 简介，2026-09-27；未听原声）
- 好环境 6–7 个核心仓并用保证金；差环境 1–2 只、敞口上限 30%（OK2）
- "15% position with a 1-2% stop (risking 15-30bps)"（OK2）
- 连亏："trade smaller and smaller and smaller until you get a couple trades going"（CMR）；2022 年最大回撤 "Like 25%"（CMR）

### 加仓
- "if it moves up a little I will add and raise my stop"；理想是"twice as big of a position and I actually have no risk on it"（CMR）
- "Never average down: Average Up."（OK2）

### 市况
- 指数 20EMA："20EMA all that matters to me. Above it...long only"（X 1927459866378924452，2025-05-27）
- 指数 50 日线拐头向下时很难做（CMR）；季节性：2 月中下旬常有回调（RAY）
- 用持仓的即时盈亏作反馈（TraderLion Clips elRa5hSQ7Jw 简介）
