## Apple keynote intraday hedge

### Takeaway
He does publish a mechanical rule, on slide 08 of 16, not only a win rate. It is a dollar-neutral open: short $1 of AAPL and long $1 of SPY at 09:30 ET, take profit at +0.25% checked every five minutes, otherwise hold to the close, no stop, 4 bp round-trip for both legs. ## His stated standard for reading a backtest

### Takeaway
The standard he repeats, in his own sentences, is to ask how many small wins one loss erases, to refuse a CAGR that is not paired with a drawdown, to split in-sample from a later sample, and to see whether one name is the whole result. Each of the three strategy posts does one of those cuts to its own headline.
